Chat room — data channel test

Messages are sent over the WebRTC data channel to the SFU, which relays them to every other connected peer. Open this page in a second tab (or another browser) to see forwarding in action.

Audio — forwarding test

Your microphone is sent to the SFU, which forwards it as raw RTP (no transcoding) to the other connected peer. Open a second tab to hear it. The meters below show live audio levels; enable playback to actually hear the other peer (headphones recommended to avoid feedback).

Video — forwarded between peers

The SFU forwards your camera to the other peer (and vice versa). Open a second tab to see the other peer's video appear on the right.
